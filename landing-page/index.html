Why We Use 4 Wavelengths (Not Just 1)

Most clinics use single-wavelength devices. We use all four — because your skin deserves precision, not approximation. Each wavelength targets different hair depths and skin types for complete, safe, permanent results.

755nm
Alexandrite

Targets high melanin absorption — perfect for fine, light hair on fair skin (Types I–III).

Best ForLight Skin, Fine Hair
808nm
Classic Diode

The universal gold standard for mid-depth follicles across most skin types (II–V).

Best ForAll Skin Types, Standard Hair
940nm
Advanced Diode

Boosts dermal heating and targets deep roots plus micro-vessels feeding the follicle.

Best ForDeep Roots, Stubborn Hair
1064nm
Nd:YAG

Low melanin absorption + deepest penetration — the safest choice for dark or tanned skin (V–VI).
